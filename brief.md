# Brief

A simple public research site for policy and procurement readers examining whether FedRAMP changes increased small-business access. Separate current representation from historical size. Make sources, confidence, denominators, and unknowns inspectable. Compare Rev5, pilot 20x, and non-pilot 20x across certified and initial implementation status; annual cohorts; equal 24-month windows.

Independent identity, restrained editorial typography, accessible controls, legible evidence tables, responsive layouts. No official logos or implied government endorsement. Standalone project, not a new variant of the FedRAMP marketing prototype lab.
