# Interpretation of the reviewed snapshot

Snapshot: October 30, 2026. See [second-pass checkpoint](second-pass.md) for research coverage and [current participation](participation.md) for generated rates. The earlier [review corrections](review-corrections.md) are retained as a superseded audit record.

All reporting uses two groups: **Documented or likely small**, and **Documented not small, likely not small, or unknown**. The latter includes unresolved cases and must not be interpreted as the prevalence of legally large businesses. Both percentages use all eligible records.

The 20x pilot certified population has a larger observed small-group share than the traditional certified population. The latest review identifies three of eight currently certified non-pilot providers as likely small, with explicit evidence qualifications. Many non-pilot initial-implementation providers have documented representations. These observations establish participation, not a causal effect of reforms.

The annual chart and rolling windows use the most recent size assessment in the snapshot, flagged explicitly. Historical evidence is retained in the downloads for audit. Current classifications must not be reported as size at entry. All-years provider totals are deduplicated; annual provider rows can overlap.

For equal rolling windows, increased certification volume alone does not establish broader small-business access. Historical size coverage, changes in listing coverage, pilot selection, class mix, ownership, and unobserved prior certification all limit interpretation. The generated [rolling-window report](recent-access.md) and CSV expose these denominators.

The most useful next evidence remains date-specific SAM representations or procurement size determinations, service-appropriate NAICS, ownership histories, unsuccessful applicants, and comparable cost/time measurements. Add these as dated source observations rather than replacing missing evidence with assumptions.
